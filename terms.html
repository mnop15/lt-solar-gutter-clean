01

Services Provided

LT Solar & Gutter Clean provides professional gutter cleaning, solar panel cleaning, and bird proofing services. All services are performed with reasonable care, skill, and in accordance with applicable safety standards.

02

Quotes & Pricing

Validity

All digital or written quotes are valid for 30 days from the date of issue.

Accuracy

Initial quotes may be based on information provided by the customer or online imagery. If we arrive on-site and find that the roof is significantly steeper, higher, multi-story, or more heavily overgrown than discussed, we reserve the right to adjust the price before starting work.

03

Weather & Rescheduling

Working on roofs is highly dependent on weather conditions. In the event of high winds, heavy rain, lightning, or extreme heat, LT Solar & Gutter Clean reserves the right to reschedule the service to the next available safe date. We are not liable for inconvenience caused by weather-related delays.

04

Service-Specific Terms

Gutter Cleaning

Our service covers the removal of debris from gutters and valleys and testing the tops of downpipes. Unblocking downpipes that are blocked underground or connected to storm-water systems is not included in standard cleaning unless explicitly quoted.

Solar Panel Cleaning

We use professional, safe methods to clean panels. We do not accept liability for pre-existing electrical faults, micro-cracks, or structural degradation of the solar array.

Bird Proofing

Bird-proofing installations come with a 1-year warranty against workmanship defects and material failure. This warranty is void if the mesh or spikes are altered, removed, or damaged by third parties, pests other than birds, or severe weather events.

05

Property Access & Utilities

Access

Customers must provide safe, clear and unobstructed access to the property and roof.

Water & Power

Customers must provide access to an outdoor water tap with standard pressure.

Safety

Pets and children must be kept indoors while work is being performed.

06

Liability & Pre-Existing Damage

Roof Integrity

LT Solar & Gutter Clean takes the utmost care when working on roofs. However, older roof tiles, particularly terracotta or brittle concrete tiles, may crack under normal walking pressure. We are not liable for tiles that were already damaged, degraded or brittle before our arrival.

Interior Water Damage

We are not liable for pre-existing interior water damage caused by blocked gutters or roof leaks that occurred before our service.

Accepted Methods

Overdue Accounts
