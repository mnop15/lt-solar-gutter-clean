Local people, doing things properly for the homes around us.

LT Solar & Gutter Clean is a local, partner-run business proudly servicing South East Melbourne.

We started this business because we kept seeing the same thing on rooftops across our own neighbourhood — solar panels quietly losing power, and gutters quietly filling up, while homeowners had no easy way to check either.

We built LT Solar & Gutter Clean to fix that, with a simple promise: honest work, visible proof, and zero mess left behind.

Every job we do comes with Before & After photos , because we believe you shouldn't have to just take our word for it.

As a local team, our reputation is built job by job, right here in the community we live in — which means we treat every roof like it's next door to our own.
