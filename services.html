Bird Proofing

Birds nesting beneath solar panels scratch wiring, build fire-hazardous nests, and drop debris that can damage your roof over time.

We install premium, heavy-duty mesh around your panels to seal the gap permanently and keep pests out.

What's included

✓ Heavy-duty, UV-stable pest mesh (not flimsy netting)
✓ Fully sealed perimeter — no re-entry points
✓ One-time install, long-term protection
✓ Add Bird Proofing to your Solar Clean or Gutter Clean booking and save on setup costs.
